Tab Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-25.

1. Who operates Tab

Tab is operated by Aasir Valji. For support or privacy questions, contact aasir.valji@hotmail.com.

Tab helps people record shared expenses and repayments. It does not transfer money, connect to banks or verify that a real payment has happened.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you use Sign in with Apple, Apple and Supabase handle authentication. Tab stores your account identifier and display name. Supabase may also receive the email address or private relay address provided by Apple.

Shared records. We store group names and settings, member names and identifiers, invitations, expense descriptions, amounts, dates, splits, notes, recorded repayments and related activity. Other participants can enter information about you, such as a placeholder member name, before you create a Tab account.

Device information. Tab stores session information and preferences on your device. If optional push notifications become available and you enable them, your Apple push device token and notification environment are used to deliver alerts.

Technical and support information. Our service providers may process request and security information, such as IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps, authentication events and error information, to operate and protect the service. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Please do not email passwords, Apple authorization codes, private keys or unnecessary financial details.

Tab does not request access to your contacts, location or photo library. It does not collect bank credentials or payment-card details.

3. How information is used

We use information to sign you in, manage groups and invitations, calculate and explain balances, synchronize shared records, support optional notifications, respond to requests and maintain service security and reliability.

Tab does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use shared expense records for advertising. The app writes basic feature-event names to the device's system log; it does not send those events to a separate analytics service.

4. Who can see information

Group members can see that group's shared records and member labels according to the app's access rules. Someone with a valid invitation can see limited group and unclaimed-member information needed to join. Keep invitation links private and share them only with intended recipients.

Supabase provides account, database and synchronization services. Apple provides sign-in and, if used, push delivery. Cloudflare Pages hosts the public website, and GitHub stores its public source files. The public website does not query or display your private group or expense records.

These providers process information required for their services under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Where a provider processes personal information on our behalf, we require protections consistent with this policy and applicable law. We may also disclose information where legally required or necessary to respond to a security incident or protect users' rights.

Our Supabase project is hosted in the United States. Providers may process information in other countries, so processing may occur outside the country where you live.

5. Retention and account deletion

Account and profile. We keep account information to provide your account. You can delete your account through Profile → Delete account. The app asks for fresh Apple confirmation. When deletion succeeds, Tab revokes its Apple access and removes your Supabase account and application profile. Account-linked push registrations and relevant invitation links are removed. If deletion fails, the app reports the failure so you can retry with fresh Apple confirmation or contact support. You do not need to contact support to initiate ordinary account deletion.

Names, notes and contributed text. Account deletion removes your profile name and account links. In shared groups, your membership is retired, its label becomes “Former member,” and another account cannot claim it.

Group names and icons, expense descriptions and notes that you created or contributed to are replaced with generic labels or removed. Copies in related activity are cleared too. Labels you entered for unclaimed members become “Member”; a member who already claimed and accepted their identity keeps their own label when the original inviter deletes their account.

Text may have multiple contributors. To avoid retaining a departing account's content, the current shared text can be cleared even if another member edited it later. Merely editing an amount without changing the text does not add text authorship under the current system. Older activity records do not distinguish every changed field, so their historical editors are treated conservatively as contributors. Information with already-removed creator or actor links is also cleaned conservatively when the revised system is introduced.

Shared financial history. If a group still has another claimed account, Tab keeps the amounts, currencies, dates, splits, repayments and stable group-member ledger identifiers needed to preserve and explain that account's balances. Account-linked creator and activity-actor references for the deleted account are removed. These records do not transfer money or prove a payment occurred. Retaining this structure is not described as a legal requirement.

The remaining context and amounts may still be recognizable to people who participated in the group. Replacing a name and removing an account link does not guarantee irreversible anonymity. If no other claimed account remains, the group and its associated application records are deleted, including archived groups and groups that have only unclaimed placeholders.

There is no automatic age-based expiry for another account's remaining shared financial history. Deleting an individual expense or repayment normally hides it from active calculations but can retain the underlying financial record and activity. Account deletion additionally applies the text cleanup described above.

Text independently entered by other people can still mention someone else. Contact aasir.valji@hotmail.com about information in another person's content or a further privacy request. We handle such requests under applicable requirements, including other participants' rights.

Logs, backups and support. Operational logs and backup copies, where maintained, follow the retention and recovery arrangements of the relevant provider and configuration. These periods are separate from the active account database and may differ by service or plan. Writing new authentication audit events into our Supabase database is currently disabled; external provider logs may still be retained. This setting does not establish whether historical database audit records remain. We do not promise that every log or backup copy disappears immediately when an account is deleted.

We use support correspondence to answer your request and handle necessary follow-up. Retention decisions consider that purpose, security needs and applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence or information in logs.

6. Website requests and device choices

Our public pages do not include analytics scripts or set their own tracking cookies. Hosting providers may process technical request information for delivery and security. An invitation URL contains its invitation code, so that code is included in the request when the link is opened in a browser. The pages do not display private group data or automatically join a group for you.

You can edit your display name and sign out in the app. Where notifications are available, you can manage them in the app and in iPhone Settings. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or shared group history.

7. Security and privacy requests

Tab uses HTTPS and server-side access controls to restrict ordinary group data to authorized members. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

For access, correction, deletion or another privacy request, email aasir.valji@hotmail.com. We may need to verify your identity without asking for your password or Apple authorization code. We will handle requests according to applicable law, including any requirements concerning shared records or other people's information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update its text and effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
