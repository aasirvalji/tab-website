What Tab stores

Tab uses Sign in with Apple and Supabase to manage your account. We store an account identifier, your display name and, when provided by Apple, an email or private relay address. Groups store their names, member labels, expenses, amounts, splits, notes and recorded repayments. If you enable push, we store an Apple push device token to deliver group updates.

Why and with whom

We use this data to provide account access, invitations, shared ledgers and optional alerts. Group members can see that group's shared records and member labels. Invite holders can see limited group and unclaimed-member information to join. Supabase processes hosted account and database data; Apple processes sign-in and push delivery. Tab does not include advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and it does not move money or connect to banks.

Visiting this website

The public website is planned to be hosted by Cloudflare Pages, with source files stored on GitHub. The pages do not make browser-side requests to your group or expense records. Hosting services may process request information, such as your IP address and the requested URL, to deliver and protect the site. An invitation URL includes its invitation code; share it only with the intended recipient. No website analytics script is included in these pages.

Your device and preferences

The app stores session information and preferences on your device. You can change your display name, turn alerts off or sign out in Profile. Apple notification permission can also be changed in iPhone Settings. No contacts, location or photo-library permission is requested.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account asks you to confirm with Apple. Successful deletion revokes Tab's Apple access and deletes your Auth account and profile. Your account's device registrations and invitation links are removed. Your member labels become “Former member” and cannot be reclaimed.

Shared expense and repayment history remains available to other group members, without your account-linked actor fields, so their balances stay correct. Free-text expense descriptions or notes entered by you or others are shared records and may still contain personal information. Please contact aasir.valji@hotmail.com about such content or other privacy requests.
