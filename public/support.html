Balances and payments

Open a person's balance to see the expense-by-expense breakdown. Tab records repayments but does not send money. One currency is used per group.

Invitations

An invite expires after seven days or after it is used. Ask a current group member for a fresh invitation. Deleted historical members cannot be claimed.

Account deletion

Open Profile → Delete account and confirm with Apple. If deletion fails, retry with a fresh Apple confirmation or contact support. Shared ledger records remain with a Former member label.

When contacting us

Include your app version, iOS version and a brief description. Never send passwords, Apple authorization codes, private keys or private financial records.
