Tab helps groups record shared expenses, explain balances and record repayments. It does not transfer money, connect to banks, provide financial advice or verify that an actual payment has occurred. “Mark as paid” records information entered by a group member; it is not proof of payment.

You are responsible for reviewing the information you enter and for checking records before making real-world payments.

3. Shared records and acceptable use

Group members can view and change shared records according to the app's permissions. Other members' entries and edits may affect the group's balances. Resolve real-world payment disagreements with the people involved; Tab does not adjudicate debts.

Tab requires internet access and depends on Apple and our hosting providers. Features may be unavailable during outages or maintenance. We cannot promise uninterrupted availability or that records supplied by users are accurate.

Review important shared records and contact support if something appears incorrect. Tab's recorded balances are based on the data entered; they are not independently verified statements of a legal obligation to pay.

5. Account deletion and shared history

Successful deletion removes your account and profile, retires your membership and removes account-linked references as explained in the Privacy Policy. Shared ledger history remains for other group members; free-text information can remain in that history. Uninstalling Tab does not delete your account.
